## Typography

Custom typeface: **Product Sans** (self-hosted TrueType, all weights + italics). Files in `fonts/`.

Weights available: Thin (100), Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700), Black (900) — each with an italic variant.

## Logos (`logos/`)

- `raremark-foundation-logo.png` — primary logo, color
- `raremark-foundation-logo-white.svg` — white/reversed logo, vector
- `raremark-foundation-icon.png` — favicon/app icon mark

## Icons & decorative graphics (`icons/`)

Mask/group graphic elements used as decorative shapes across the site (`Mask-Group-*`, `Group-3604`, `1696092784774@2x`).

## Partner / affiliate logos (`partner-logos/`)

Logos of partner organizations shown on the site (Ministry of Health Consortium, Digimark, Banz Medcare, Gulu Regional Referral Hospital, Kampala MRI Centre, Masters Bethlehem, TSCA, Hips for Africa). Not RareMark's own brand mark — flag to the designer as third-party marks, included only for layout reference.

## Photography (`images/`)

Editorial/program photography (event photos, program imagery) pulled at their largest available resolution (originals or largest generated size, typically 2048px on the long edge).

## Notes for the designer

- All assets are the largest resolution publicly served by the site; WordPress-generated thumbnail sizes were deduped out, keeping one file per source image.
- `docs/site.css` is the site's full compiled stylesheet, useful for spot-checking exact spacing/color values if anything above is ambiguous.
